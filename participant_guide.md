# Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon Participant Guide

Welcome to the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon (the “Hackathon”)! We’re thrilled to have you on board.
This guide is your all-in-one resource for the event, including schedule, rules, technical resources, problem statements, judging information, and more. Please read this carefully; most answers can be found here.

---

## 1. Your Goal: Hackathon Problem Statements

Your project must build towards one of the following problem statements:

* **Build for Your World** - Bring Meta's models into your domain. Solve a problem you know firsthand, free up time and mental space, and make something possible that wasn't before.
* **Push the Frontier** - Use Meta's models to tackle challenges in software, robotics, manufacturing, energy, materials and beyond. Give a machine a new capability, accelerate a scientific experiment, or unlock a better way to understand and control a physical system. Show us what becomes possible when these models meet the real world.

Your project must incorporate Meta's models. Use the [Muse Spark series](https://dev.meta.ai/models/muse-spark), [Muse Wonder](https://dev.meta.ai/models/muse-wonder), [Muse Image](https://dev.meta.ai/models/muse-image), [Muse Voice Transcribe](https://dev.meta.ai/models/muse-voice-transcribe) and [SAM 3.1](https://dev.meta.ai/models/sam-3-1) through [Meta Model API](https://dev.meta.ai/products/meta-model-api), run Meta open weights models [Muse Glimmer](https://dev.meta.ai/models/muse-glimmer) and [Muse Spark Open](https://dev.meta.ai/models/muse-spark-open), and code faster with [Muse Code](https://dev.meta.ai/lp/muse-code) coding agent.

## 4. Hackathon Rules

* **Open source:** Repositories must be public.
* **Team size:** Maximum of 4 participants per team. Solo participants are allowed.
* **Demo requirements:** Your demo must only highlight the specific features, code, and functionality built during the Hackathon. Judges must clearly identify original contributions.
* **New work only:** Must be built during the event. Startups may build on existing codebases, but must clearly identify what was newly developed.
* **Banned projects (Anti-Projects):**
  * ❌ Any project using AI to generate and give medical advice (e.g. AI Mental Health Advisor, skin blemish detector, etc.)
  * ❌ Basic RAG Applications
  * ❌ Simple Image Analyzers
  * ❌ Simple “AI for Education” Chatbots
  * ❌ AI Job Application Screener
  * ❌ AI Nutrition Coach
  * ❌ Personality Analyzers
  * ❌ Sports analyzers or coaches
  * ❌ Anything political or involving war

## 8. Judging Process & Rubric

### 3 Stages:
* **Stage One:** Pass/Fail viability (fits theme, reasonably uses Meta Model API / open weights models).
* **Stage Two:** Scored on a 0-10 scale across 4 weighted pillars:
  * **Impact (30%):** Real-world potential, who benefits, does it solve a firsthand or frontier problem.
  * **Use of Meta AI Tooling (25%):** Creative use beyond basic integration; reveals surprising capabilities.
  * **Demo (25%):** Working, impressive live demo; engaging 3-minute video.
  * **Technical Depth & Execution (20%):** Sound engineering, thoughtful refinement, crafted architecture.
* **Stage Three:** Top 5 projects per track move to final round live evaluation.
